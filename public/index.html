Arm live trading?

  • The bot will trade the real USDC in its wallet until disarm, a restart, or a risk limit intervenes.
  • It needs USDC + a tiny bit of ETH once (Ethereum mainnet). After that it buys its own gas with USDC (small amounts, capped per day).
  • It may buy tokens that pass the pool, history and strategy checks. Long-tail candidates also pass a simulated round trip. New buys are capped by aggregate long-tail exposure.
  • Losses are possible. It can hold up to 3 tokens at once (max 45% of equity each). Risk limits: ~2.5% of equity per trade, daily loss stop 6%, max drawdown halt 25%.
  • Back up the private key first. See the README for storage and export instructions.
